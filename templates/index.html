About Us

About NyayaSetu

Many legal documents are complex and hard to understand, especially for rural citizens. NyayaSetu simplifies these documents into plain language, highlights risky clauses, and provides audio explanations in regional languages. Our mission is to empower everyone to understand their rights and make informed decisions with confidence.
